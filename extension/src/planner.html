    Tips

    Each column is a term. Past terms come from your AMIS grades, future terms are the earliest plan that respects prerequisites, the sems each course is actually offered, and your unit cap.

    Select a course to see its full prerequisite chain, what it unlocks and how many courses a failure would block. Mark a course failed to see the retake and the delay.

    Courses marked Critical, joined by thin maroon lines, are your critical path. These are the courses that would delay graduation if you slip, even by one term.

    Offerings come from recent AMIS class listings. FE cards are free elective placeholders. Any course you pass outside your checklist fills one, and they are never critical.

    Reset the plan?

    This clears the courses you marked passed or failed, the courses you moved, and your petitions. Your AMIS grades stay.
