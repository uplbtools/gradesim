How to use Elbi GradeSim

Leave a course out of your GWA

Select Leave out of GWA under a course to drop it from the calculation, for example a course from a program you shifted out of. NSTP, HK and PE never count, and grades like INC and DRP are skipped on their own.

Scholar status

A semester shows University Scholar at a GWA of 1.45 or better and College Scholar at 1.75 or better, when you carried at least 15 units with no 4.00, 5.00 or INC.

Target Latin honors

In the What if tab, pick a Latin honor such as cum laude. GradeSim shows the average grade you need in your remaining units to reach it.

Backup and restore

Export JSON saves your grades and settings to a file. Import JSON restores them later or on another device.

Clear my data

Delete your grades and every setting GradeSim saved in this browser. Export JSON first if you want a copy.

Terms of Use and Privacy Notice

About this tool

Elbi GradeSim is an independent student-made browser extension for UP Los Baños students to track and simulate their GWA. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the University of the Philippines (UP) System, UPLB, or the UPLB AMIS portal.

1. Privacy and data

Your academic data stays on your device. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere, and there are no analytics or external servers.

2. Saved on your device

All grade data is saved only inside your browser. Uninstalling the extension or clearing your browser data permanently removes your stored grades.

3. Accuracy

All GWA calculations and target simulations are unofficial estimates. They may not account for incomplete (INC), dropped (DRP), or deferred grades. Always check your official records with the UPLB Office of the University Registrar (OUR).

4. Responsibility and IP

You are responsible for what you do with this tool, and the developer assumes no liability. UP and UPLB marks belong to the UP System and appear here only to name the university.
